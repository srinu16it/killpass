# The Five Traps

Generic "verify this" prompts miss patterned deceptions. killpass tests
five by name, learned from real, documented failures in a research system:

1. **Title vs body**, the headline says "Raises," the document says
   "Updates" or "Reaffirms." (Killed a real stock nomination: the release
   title contradicted its own numbers.)
2. **Raise inside a cut**, one number goes up while another goes down for
   the same period, in the same document. (A real company raised revenue
   guidance and cut profit guidance in one press release.)
3. **Borrowed good news**, the positive event is funded by new debt or
   one-time items disclosed elsewhere. (A real "raise" turned out to be a
   one-time tax benefit; another company's buybacks ran on a fresh loan.)
4. **Recycled news**, the "new" $2.7B contract was a consolidation of
   contracts already held. (Real case: the market moved +0.6% because it
   knew.)
5. **Stale claim**, the sources predate the claim's timeframe.

Every verdict includes the checklist results, even when the claim passes , 
users see what was tested, not just the answer.
